The Sound of Egypt

The Oud

A classic, pear-shaped stringed instrument commonly used in Middle Eastern music. It plays a central role across traditional Egyptian compositions.

The Darbuka

A goblet-shaped drum capable of producing rapid, sharp beats. It acts as the rhythmic backbone for Egyptian folk and dance tracks.

Umm Kulthum

Known as the "Star of the East," she is the most celebrated Egyptian vocalist in history, famous for her powerful voice and deeply emotional epics.
