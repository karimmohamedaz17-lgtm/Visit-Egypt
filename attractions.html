Must-Visit Landmarks

The Pyramids of Giza

Located on the outskirts of Cairo, these ancient structures are the last remaining wonder of the ancient world, guarded by the mysterious Great Sphinx.

Luxor Temple

An open-air museum located on the east bank of the Nile River, known for its massive statues, towering columns, and historical avenues.

The Grand Egyptian Museum

A world-class architectural marvel housing thousands of ancient Egyptian artifacts, including the legendary treasures of King Tutankhamun.
