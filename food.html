Traditional Egyptian Dishes

Koshary

The national dish of Egypt. It is a hearty mix of rice, macaroni, lentils, and chickpeas, topped with a savory tomato sauce, garlic vinegar, and crispy fried onions.

Ful Medames

A staple Egyptian breakfast made of cooked fava beans flavored with olive oil, cumin, garlic, lemon juice, and served alongside warm baladi bread.

Ta'ameya (Egyptian Falafel)

Unlike standard falafel made with chickpeas, Egyptian ta'ameya is made from crushed fava beans and fresh herbs, making it incredibly light and fluffy on the inside.
